Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 May 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how Ashton Ryan and Zavier Darcy ("we", "us", or "our") collect, use, and protect your information when you use the Crisper AI mobile application ("the App"). By using the App you agree to this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide directly:

  • Email address and password when creating an account
  • Body statistics including age, weight, height, and fitness goals
  • Food and meal logs you create or scan
  • Progress photos you choose to upload
  • Weight log entries
  • Custom meals and shopping list preferences

Information collected automatically:

  • Barcode scan data used to look up food nutrition information
  • Camera images used for food scanning — processed to identify meals and macros, not stored on our servers
  • App usage data for improving functionality

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Create and manage your account
  • Provide personalised meal tracking, macro calculations, and nutrition insights
  • Display your progress over time
  • Generate shopping lists and meal suggestions
  • Respond to support requests

We do not use your data for advertising and we do not sell your data to any third party.

3. How We Store Your Information

Your account and data are stored securely using Supabase, a third-party cloud database provider with servers located in the United States. Data stored locally on your device remains on your device and is not transmitted to our servers unless you are signed in to an account.

4. Camera and Photo Library Access

The App requests camera access solely to scan food items and log meals. Images are processed on-device or sent to an AI service to identify food and estimate nutrition — they are not stored permanently on our servers.

The App requests photo library access solely to allow you to save or retrieve progress photos. We do not share camera or photo data with any third party for marketing purposes.

5. Children's Privacy

Crisper AI is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at crisperaiapp@gmail.com and we will delete it promptly.

6. Your Rights

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Delete your account and all data — via Profile → Delete Account in the App, or by emailing us
  • Withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account

We handle your data in accordance with the Australian Privacy Act 1988 and the Australian Privacy Principles.

7. Third-Party Services

  • Supabase — authentication and data storage
  • OpenAI / AI food analysis — meal and food recognition from images

8. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, all associated data is permanently removed from our systems within 30 days.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
