Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 September 2026 · replaces the version dated 2 May 2025

Crisper AI is operated by Ashton Ryan and Zavier Darcy ("we", "us", or "our"). You can reach us at crisperaiapp@gmail.com.

This policy explains what we collect, where it goes, and what you can do about it. We have tried to describe what the App actually does rather than what a privacy policy usually says — including the things we don't collect, and the one limitation we have chosen to live with.

  • We store your account, your body stats and your food, weight, water and activity logs so they sync between your devices.
  • We never store your photos. A fridge or meal photo is sent to our AI provider, analysed, and discarded. It is not saved on our servers at any point.
  • We run no analytics and no advertising. There is no tracking software in the App. We do not sell or share your data for marketing, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • Deleting your account deletes your data immediately, not eventually.
  • The minimum age is 13, and the App deliberately refuses to put anyone under 18 into a calorie deficit.

3. Photos and AI analysis

This is the part people most want to understand, so here it is in full. When you scan your fridge or a meal:

  • The photo is resized down on your device first, so a smaller image than the one your camera took is what leaves your phone (never more than 1024px on the longest edge, and 768px for meal scans)
  • It is sent over an encrypted connection to our API proxy, hosted on Railway
  • The proxy forwards it to Anthropic, which runs the Claude model that identifies the food and estimates nutrition
  • The result comes back as text. The photo is not written to disk by us at any stage and is not retained after the request completes

Some clarifications, because the previous policy was imprecise:

  • Food photo analysis always happens off your device. It cannot be done locally. Only barcode decoding runs on-device
  • Our AI provider is Anthropic, not OpenAI. The earlier policy named the wrong company. Anthropic processes the image to answer that one request and, under its commercial API terms, does not use it to train its models
  • Nutrition estimates are estimates. The App deliberately shows ranges and confidence levels rather than false precision. Do not treat them as medical or dietary advice

4. Barcode scanning

Barcodes are decoded on your device. Once decoded, the barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts, an independent open database, to look up product nutrition information. Only the barcode number is sent — no account information, and no photograph.

5. Why we use your information

  • To create and maintain your account, and sync your data across your devices
  • To calculate your calorie and macronutrient targets from the body information you provide
  • To identify food from the photos you choose to scan, and estimate its nutrition
  • To generate meal suggestions, recipes and reports from what is in your logs
  • To operate, secure and rate-limit our service, and control our AI costs
  • To manage subscriptions and honour referral discounts

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling for third parties, or any automated decision-making with a legal effect on you.

8. Health information and your consent

Your weight, body measurements, dietary preferences and fitness goals are health information, which is "sensitive information" under the Australian Privacy Act and a "special category" under the GDPR. Both give it a higher level of protection.

We collect it only with your consent, which you give by entering it during onboarding, and only to calculate your nutrition targets and personalise suggestions. You can withdraw that consent at any time by deleting your account. We never disclose health information to anyone other than the providers in section 6 who are necessary to run the App.

9. How we protect it

  • All traffic uses encrypted HTTPS connections
  • Your database rows are protected by row-level security, enforced per user by our database. Every read and write is scoped to your own account identifier, so one account cannot reach another's data
  • Our AI proxy will not accept a request without a cryptographically verified sign-in token. It pins the AI model and caps request sizes server-side, so a modified copy of the App cannot change them
  • Sign-in tokens are stored in the iOS Keychain rather than in web storage, and are excluded from device backups
  • Our providers encrypt data at rest

One limitation we want to be straight about. Sign-in tokens are valid for up to one hour and are verified by signature rather than checked against a list on every request. If you sign out or delete your account you cannot obtain a new token — but a token already issued keeps working until it expires, at most one hour later. We accepted this because the window is short, cannot be extended, and is tightly rate-limited. No security design is perfect and we would rather tell you than imply otherwise.

10. How long we keep it, and deletion

We keep your information while your account exists.

Deleting your account deletes your data immediately. Go to Profile → Delete Account in the App, or email us at crisperaiapp@gmail.com and we will do it for you. This removes your account and all associated records — profile, meals, favourites, scan history, custom meals, weight, water and activity logs — in a single operation, not on a 30-day timer as our previous policy stated.

Two things survive briefly and for narrow reasons:

  • Server logs (timestamp, endpoint, status, IP, truncated account id) persist in our hosting provider's rolling log retention. They contain no personal content
  • Apple's purchase records are governed by Apple's own policy; we cannot delete those

Because we never store your photos, there are no photos to delete.

11. Accessing and correcting your information

Most of your information is directly visible and editable inside the App — your profile, your logs and your history. You can correct it there at any time.

If you want a copy of everything we hold, or want something corrected that the App will not let you change, email crisperaiapp@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days. There is no charge. If we refuse a request we will tell you why in writing.

12. Age requirements and under-18s

Crisper AI is not for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13, and if we discover we have, we will delete it.

If you are 13 to 17, please use Crisper AI with a parent or guardian's knowledge and involvement, and talk to them before making changes to how you eat. In some countries, including much of the EU and UK, a parent or guardian must consent on your behalf — by using the App you confirm that consent has been given where it is required.

One deliberate design choice you should know about: the App will not put anyone under 18 into a calorie deficit. If you are under 18 and select a fat-loss goal, your target is raised to your estimated maintenance level instead. Goals that involve eating more are unaffected. Growing bodies should not be calorie-restricted by an app, and an earlier version of Crisper AI got this wrong.

If you are a parent or guardian and want your child's account and data removed, email us and we will do it.
